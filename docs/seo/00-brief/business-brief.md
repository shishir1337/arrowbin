## Goals
- Rank top 1 to 3 for every good-volume, commercially valuable keyword we can realistically win.
- Traffic, and above all **leads** (project briefs, booked calls).
- Visibility in AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Copilot).
- Primary market: USA (plus UK/CA/AU and other English-speaking buyers hiring remote/offshore teams). Secondary: Bangladesh (local businesses, e-commerce) and the Tampa Bay / Florida area.

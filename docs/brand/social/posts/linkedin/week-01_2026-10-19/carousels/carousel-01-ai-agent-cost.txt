What an AI agent really costs a small business (2026)

POST TEXT (from the Arrowbin Page):

The AI model is usually the cheapest part of an AI agent.

We priced one workload, 3,000 customer conversations a month, on 8 models using each provider's own price page. Model fees came to between $5 and $216 a month. That's 0.17 to 7.2 cents a conversation.

The real budget is set by three questions:
1. How many of your systems does the agent connect to?
2. How much can it do without a person checking?
3. Who keeps it accurate after launch?

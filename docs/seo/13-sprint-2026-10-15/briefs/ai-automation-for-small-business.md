**Overall gaps:**

- Error cost (what a wrong answer costs) is almost never part of the "what first" decision.
- Human-in-the-loop is either missing or a generic "review before sending"; nobody describes levels of autonomy or when to move between them.
- No page mentions prompt injection, even though inbox and chat automations read text written by strangers.
- No per-use-case buy vs build guidance.
- Almost no official adoption data.

## Our angle
An engineer's "where to start" guide. Eight workflows that work in 2026, each with what it saves, the main risk, the human step and buy or build, then a 4-factor scoring sheet (volume × repetitiveness × error cost × data availability, each 1 to 5, multiplied so one weak factor sinks the score) with an illustrative worked example. Add the autonomy ladder (shadow, approve, sample, act alone) and a safety checklist grounded in OWASP's prompt-injection guidance. Money questions go to the cost post.

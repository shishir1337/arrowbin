For anyone comparing development teams in different countries: we worked out median developer pay from Stack Overflow's 2025 and 2026 surveys (25,416 developers).

Median yearly pay:
• United States: $155,000
• United Kingdom: $95,299
• Germany: $83,340
• Poland: $55,040
• India: $19,761
• Pakistan: $9,937

Pay varies a lot inside each country too. India's middle half runs from $8,879 to $39,521.

Bangladesh had 92 qualifying respondents, under our threshold of 100, so we don't publish a figure for it.

Does anything here surprise you?

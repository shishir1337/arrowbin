Your AI-built app works. That doesn't mean it's safe.

Three checks you can run today, before you take another payment:
1. Open your app logged out and ask for private data. You should get nothing back.
2. Sign in as one user and change the ID to another user's record. If it loads, your access rules are wrong.
3. Search your live site's JavaScript for service_role and sk_live. A secret key should never be there.

# SERP analysis: Arrowbin target keywords

Research date: 2026-09-29. Market: US (English).

## Method and limits (read first)

- Results come from a US web search API plus direct fetches of selected ranking pages. The API returns about 9 organic results per query in close to Google order. Treat positions as **approximate** (plus or minus 2 places), not a rank-tracker reading.
- Google and Bing result pages could not be fetched directly (Google served an error page, Bing returned unrelated results). So **People Also Ask boxes, AI Overviews, local packs and video carousels were not observed directly**. Where this doc names a SERP feature, it is marked:
  - **Observed**: seen in the returned results (for example a Yelp or Built In local list, a directory at #1).
  - **Likely**: an informed judgement from the query type (for example cost and "how long" questions nearly always trigger an AI Overview and PAA in 2026). Verify with a manual incognito US search or a SERP API (DataForSEO, Semrush) before relying on it.
- Content traits (word count, FAQs, tables, author, date) were **measured** for pages fetched (marked "fetched") and **inferred from titles/snippets** for the rest.
- Figures quoted from ranking pages (cost ranges, rates) are those pages' claims, not verified facts. Do not republish them as Arrowbin statistics without citing the source.
- Volumes and keyword difficulty scores are not in this doc; they belong in `04-keywords`. Difficulty here is a qualitative judgement from who ranks.

Difficulty scale for a young, low-authority domain:
- **Low**: thin or small-site results, few directories, winnable in months with one strong page.
- **Medium**: mix of mid-size agencies and blogs; needs a clearly better page plus some links.
- **High**: large agencies (ScienceSoft, Itransition, Appinventiv, N-iX) and/or one directory; a long-term target.
- **Very High**: directories (Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms) and marketplaces (Upwork, Toptal) own most of page one; ranking the arrowbin.com page is unrealistic in the near term, so the play is to get **listed on** those directories instead.

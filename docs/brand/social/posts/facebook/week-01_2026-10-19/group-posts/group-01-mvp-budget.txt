Founders: before you compare quotes for an MVP, ask how many weeks it takes.

The formula we use: build weeks × team size × weekly rate, plus launch costs and the first year of running costs. The first part is nearly all of it.

On a $60,000 SaaS MVP built over about 14 weeks, that's roughly $4,300 a week. So each week of scope you add costs about another $4,300, and each week you cut saves it.

What adds weeks most often: more core workflows, a second user role, each integration, extra platforms, design depth and slow decisions.

If you've built an MVP, how close did the final cost come to the first quote?

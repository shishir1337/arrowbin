# Winning content formats in this niche

Based on SERP checks run on 2026-09-29 (US results) and the competitor sitemaps described in `competitor-profiles.md`. "Ranks" means the page showed up in the first page of results we pulled. That tells us what Google rewards, but it is not a position-tracking measurement.

## 1. "Top / best X companies in <place or niche>" listicles (self-inclusive)
**Evidence:** This is the most common format and the one AI answers cite most. In the Arrowbin LLM visibility test, ChatGPT's answer to "best custom software development companies 2026" cited agency-owned listicles (gojilabs.com, lowcode.agency, esparkinfo.com) ahead of directories. Every Bangladesh peer runs one that ranks itself #1.
- https://brainstation-23.com/top-software-companies-in-bangladesh/ (BS23 #1)
- https://vivasoftltd.com/best-software-companies-in-bangladesh/ (Vivasoft #1)
- https://riseuplabs.com/best-software-development-company-in-bangladesh/
- https://www.ontiktechnology.com/blog/top-software-companies-in-bangladesh
- https://www.simform.com/blog/top-custom-software-development-companies/ (Simform #3 of 17, about 9K words, 12-question FAQ)
- https://www.upsilonit.com/blog/best-mvp-development-companies-in-usa
- https://www.fingent.com/blog/top-custom-software-development-companies-in-the-usa/

**Why it wins:** it matches "best X" intent exactly, it is easy for an LLM to extract, and it earns links from the firms listed.

**Note for Arrowbin:** the BD version of this SERP is crowded with self-serving lists. A differentiated version would disclose its criteria and show the data behind each pick (Clutch rating, review count, minimum project size, rates, focus). Whether to list Arrowbin in its own list is a judgment call, and the page must disclose it either way (Kaz does).

## 2. "How much does X cost" guides
**Evidence:** these are the #1 organic format for cost queries. Aalpha has about 155 cost URLs and Space-O has 39.
- https://andersenlab.com/blueprint/custom-software-development-costs-in-2026 (#1 for "how much does custom software development cost 2026")
- https://keyholesoftware.com/cost-custom-software-development/
- https://soltech.net/how-much-does-custom-software-development-cost/
- https://www.scnsoft.com/software-development/costs
- https://appinventiv.com/guide/mobile-app-development-cost/
- https://www.upsilonit.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-an-mvp
- https://www.businessofapps.com/app-developers/research/app-development-cost/

**Winning anatomy:**
- tiered price ranges (simple / mid / enterprise)
- a cost breakdown by phase or feature
- a regional rate table
- a named author with credentials (plus a reviewer on Aalpha)
- an "updated <month year>" date
- an FAQ block
- a link to a calculator

The "cost to build an app like <Uber>" sub-format has its own large SERP: https://www.cleveroad.com/blog/uber-like-app-development-cost/, https://appinventiv.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-develop-an-app-like-careem-uber/.

## 3. Cost calculators / estimators
**Evidence:**
- ScienceSoft (275 calculator URLs): https://www.scnsoft.com/software-development/costs/calculator
- Space-O (19): https://www.spaceotechnologies.com/software-cost-calculator/
- Vivasoft: https://vivasoftltd.com/mvp-cost-calculator/
- Upsilon: https://www.upsilonit.com/mvp-calculator
- Vention: https://ventionteams.com/project-cost-calculator
- Glorium: https://gloriumtech.com/pricing/software-development-cost-calculator/

The SERP for "software development cost calculator" is entirely calculator pages.

**Gap:** every competitor calculator we checked (ScienceSoft, Space-O, Upsilon, Vivasoft) hides the number behind an email form. An ungated calculator that shows a range on the page is a real differentiator. It is also more citable by AI engines, because the range sits in the HTML.

## 4. Offshore rates by country / "outsourcing to <country>" guides
**Evidence:**
- https://www.aalpha.net/articles/offshore-software-development-hourly-rates/
- https://qubit-labs.com/average-hourly-rates-offshore-development-services-software-development-costs-guide/
- https://www.fullstack.com/labs/resources/blog/software-development-price-guide-hourly-rate-comparison
- Bangladesh-specific: https://kaz.com.bd/outsource-software-development-to-bangladesh, https://vivasoftltd.com/software-development-outsourcing-to-bangladesh/, https://impalaintech.com/blog/software-development-outsourcing-to-bangladesh/, https://www.kaz.com.bd/blog/offshore-software-development-bangladesh

**Watch out:** these pages recycle unsourced statistics (e.g. a "$288 average monthly salary" and "600,000 IT professionals" appear in the snippets without primary sources). A version that cites primary data (BASIS, the ICT Division, Clutch rate filters) would stand out, especially for AI engines that prefer attributable numbers.

## 5. "Hire X developers" pages
**Evidence:**
- Platforms own the head terms: https://www.toptal.com/hire-react-developers, https://lemon.io/hire/react-developers/, https://arc.dev/hire-developers/reactjs
- Agencies still rank: https://www.mindk.com/services/hire-react-developers/, https://www.weblineglobal.com/hire/react-developers/
- Programmatic volume: Riseup Labs has about 492 hire pages and Space-O has 62.

**Verdict:** high competition and, for Arrowbin's size, a staff-augmentation sales motion it may not want. A handful of pages matched to its real stack (Next.js, React, Node, Flutter or React Native, Laravel/WordPress/Shopify for ecommerce) would be defensible. Hundreds of templated pages would not.

## 6. City / country landing pages
**Evidence:**
- Tampa: https://dragonpoint.com/custom-software-development-tampa/, https://binary-studio.com/custom-software-development-tampa/
- St. Pete: https://2muchcoffee.com/software-development-company-st-petersburg, https://gaazzeebo.net/locations/st-petersburg, https://ringomedia.com/florida/st-petersburg/software-development
- Country pages: Simform (17 US cities), Brain Station 23 (UK/UAE/AU), Aalpha (UK/CA/DE/AU)

**Pattern that wins locally:**
- the city in the H1
- the local address
- LocalBusiness + FAQPage schema
- local testimonials
- published price ranges (only Gaazzeebo does this)

Seven of the nine St. Pete results were landing pages, many of them templated and non-local.

## 8. Buyer-guide / "how to choose" posts
**Evidence:** https://www.designrush.com/agency/software-development/trends/how-to-choose-software-development-company, https://ventionteams.com/blog/select-right-software-development-company, https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbestechcouncil/2025/10/14/how-to-choose-a-software-development-company/

The SERP is saturated, and Forbes and DesignRush sit at the top. Arrowbin already has this post. It should be the checklist-plus-template version (an RFP template, vendor scorecard) and not another generic list.

## 9. Statistics pages
**Evidence:** https://www.scnsoft.com/healthcare/telemedicine-statistics, `itransition.com/software-development/statistics` (per sitemap).

Stats pages are link and citation magnets. They are a strong GEO format, but they are only credible when every number is sourced.

## 10. Case studies with a "key numbers" block
**Evidence:** Upsilon's `/cases/<slug>` format (key numbers, team, stack, named testimonial) and Simform's business-impact metrics. Most BD peers (Vivasoft, Riseup, SJ Innovation, Kaz) publish case studies with no outcome metrics.

## Formats and signals common to the pages that win
- **Tiered ranges in tables:** LLMs quote tables.
- **Named author with credentials, "updated" date, reviewer:** Aalpha, Space-O, ScienceSoft, Simform.
- **FAQ block on every commercial page:** Space-O and BS23 use FAQPage schema. Many others show an FAQ without the schema.
- **`llms.txt`:** present on Itransition, Simform, Andersen, Aalpha, Space-O, BS23, Kaz, Riseup, Technext and 2muchcoffee. Absent on ScienceSoft, Upsilon, Vivasoft, SJ Innovation, Sourcetoad and RevStar. There is no evidence here that it drives rankings. It is table stakes at worst.
- **Expert/author hubs:** ScienceSoft (about 57 `/experts` pages) and Itransition (33).

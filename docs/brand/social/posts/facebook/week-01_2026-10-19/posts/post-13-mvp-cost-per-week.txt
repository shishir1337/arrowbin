What will your MVP cost? Count the build weeks before you ask about hourly rates.

The formula we use: build weeks × team size × weekly rate, plus launch costs and the first year of running costs. The first part is nearly all of it.

On our worked example, a $60,000 SaaS MVP built over about 14 weeks, the team costs about $4,300 a week. Every week of scope you add costs roughly another $4,300, and every week you cut saves it.

What adds weeks: more core workflows, a second user role, each integration, extra platforms, design depth and slow decisions.

What does a software developer earn in different countries? We worked it out from Stack Overflow's 2025 and 2026 developer surveys (25,416 developers).

Median yearly pay:
• United States: $155,000
• United Kingdom: $95,299
• Germany: $83,340
• Poland: $55,040
• India: $19,761
• Pakistan: $9,937

The US median is 7.8 times India's. The gap narrows with experience: Indian developers with 10+ years earn about a quarter of what their US peers earn, against about a tenth for those with under five years.

Bangladesh had 92 qualifying respondents, under our threshold of 100, so we don't publish a figure for it.

# Link building and digital PR plan (6 months)

Start date: October 2026. Baseline: [backlink-baseline.md](./backlink-baseline.md). Opportunity list: [link-opportunities.csv](./link-opportunities.csv). Citations: [../08-local/citations.csv](../08-local/citations.csv).

## Principles
- **No bought links, no PBNs, no link exchanges at scale.** A small agency site with zero authority is exactly the profile that gets hurt by manipulative links. Every tactic below is earnable.
- **Brand mentions count too.** AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews) lean on third-party mentions, reviews and directory pages. A nofollow Clutch profile with 10 reviews is worth more for AI visibility and leads than a random dofollow blog link.
- **Anchor text:** keep it branded ("Arrowbin", "Arrowbin LLC") or naked URL for 80%+ of links. Never ask for exact-match anchors like "software company in dhaka".
- **Quality bar for any target:** real audience, relevant topic (software, startups, e-commerce, Bangladesh business, Tampa Bay business), not a "write for us" link farm.

## What realistically moves the needle (priority order)
1. **Client footer credits** (6+ sites missing, 2 already live). Fast, free, relevant, followed.
2. **Reviews + B2B directory profiles** (Clutch, GoodFirms, Hostinger Agency Directory, Crunchbase, LinkedIn). Mostly nofollow, but they rank themselves and feed AI answers.
3. **Expert quotes via Qwoted / Help a B2B Writer / Featured.** The most scalable route to editorial links for a founder with genuine expertise (software costs, MVP timelines, e-commerce builds in Bangladesh, offshore development).
4. **Two linkable assets**: a cost calculator and a sourced "Bangladesh e-commerce statistics 2026" page.
5. **Community presence in Dhaka**: BASIS, e-CAB, BdOSN, university sponsorships, GDG talks.
6. **Open-source payment integration kit** for Next.js + Bangladeshi gateways.
7. **Guest posts and podcasts**, a few high-quality ones, not volume.

## Weekly time budget (founder + 1 helper)
- Founder: 2 hours/week (journalist quotes, podcasts, talks, approving outreach).
- Helper/marketer: 4 to 6 hours/week (listings, outreach emails, tracking sheet).

## Tracking sheet columns
date, target, url, type, contact, status (pitched/replied/live/declined), link url, rel (follow/nofollow), anchor, notes. Review monthly alongside GSC Links.

## What not to do
- Paid "guest post" marketplaces and Fiverr link packages.
- Sitewide footer links with keyword anchors from unrelated sites.
- Listing the virtual office on Google Maps (see local plan).
- Mass directory submission tools (hundreds of low-quality directories).
- Reciprocal link schemes with other agencies.

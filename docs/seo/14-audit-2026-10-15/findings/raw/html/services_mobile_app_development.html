(01)Overview

What is Mobile App Development?

Mobile app development is designing and building native or cross-platform apps for iOS and Android that are fast, intuitive and ready to scale as your user base grows. Arrowbin builds them for startups and businesses taking a product to mobile, and a focused MVP usually reaches the stores in 10–16 weeks.

(03)Device features

Everything the phone can do

The features that make an app worth installing instead of a bookmarked website. We build them natively, on both platforms.

  • Push notifications

    Timely, segmented nudges that bring people back, not spam.

  • Offline mode

    Works on a bad connection and syncs when it's back.

  • Camera & scanning

    Photos, documents, QR and barcode scanning built in.

  • Maps & location

    Live tracking, geofencing and nearby search.

  • Biometric login

    Face ID and fingerprint sign-in, secure and instant.

  • In-app payments

    Apple Pay, Google Pay, cards, wallets and subscriptions.

  • Real-time chat

    Messaging, support chat and live updates.

  • Deep links

    Links that open the right screen, from email, ads or SMS.

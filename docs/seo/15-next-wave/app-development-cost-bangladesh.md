## FAQs
1. **How much does a simple Android app cost in Bangladesh?** One Bangladeshi vendor's published range for a basic Android app is Tk 1.5–3 lakh, about $1,215–2,430 at mid-October 2026 rates. A login screen, a few content screens and push notifications fit that budget; payments, chat and an admin panel push it higher.
2. **What hourly rate do Bangladeshi app agencies charge?** Most Bangladeshi app agencies listed on Clutch show $25–49 an hour, with a few under $25 and a few at $50–99. Shops that price for local clients often quote far less per hour, so compare line-item quotes for the same scope rather than rates alone.
3. **Should I pay a Bangladeshi developer in Taka or dollars?** Pay in the currency you earn in if you can, and fix the exchange rate or the currency in the contract. A Taka quote for a foreign client should state whether VAT is included.
4. **Who should own the Google Play and App Store accounts?** You should. Open both developer accounts in your company's name and add the developer as a user, so the app and its reviews stay yours if you change teams.
5. **Why is my local quote so much lower than an agency's?** Usually because it covers less: little design, no automated tests, a shared template backend, or no bug-fix period. Ask each vendor to list what's included line by line.
6. **How long does it take to build an app in Bangladesh?** One Bangladeshi vendor publishes 1–2 months for a basic app and 6–12 months for an enterprise one. New personal Google Play accounts also need a 14-day closed test with 12 testers before launch, so plan for it.
7. **How much does app maintenance cost per year?** Plan on 15–20% of the build cost a year by our estimate; one Bangladeshi vendor publishes 15–25%. It covers OS updates, security fixes, server costs and small changes.
8. **Can a Bangladeshi team build an app for a US or UK company?** Yes, and many export agencies do. Check time-zone overlap, who signs the contract, how code is handed over, and proof of similar work, the same as you would anywhere.

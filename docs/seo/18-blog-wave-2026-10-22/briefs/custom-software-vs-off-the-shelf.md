### What they miss (our angle)

1. **Nobody uses real, current seat prices.** Every cost model uses invented per-user figures. We use list prices opened today from HubSpot, Salesforce, monday.com, Airtable and Zapier, and show the math.
2. **Nobody says plainly that off-the-shelf wins most of the time.** They are agencies selling builds. We say buy accounting, payroll, email and a standard CRM, and show a worked example where buying wins at 40 staff and the hybrid wins at 80.
3. **The hybrid route is a footnote at best.** We make it the main recommendation, with a picture of what "buy the core, build the layer" looks like.
4. **Statistics are unsourced or mis-sourced.** We cite Zylo (unused licenses) and Vertice (SaaS price inflation) from their own pages, and O*NET/BLS for the wage behind re-keying time.
5. **Written for enterprises.** Ours is for a 10 to 200 person US business, with a checklist an owner can run in ten minutes.

Freshness: most competitors carry 2026 update dates but no 2026 prices. A dated post with today's list prices has an edge.

## Worked example (illustrative, computed 22 Oct 2026)

Hypothetical company, 40 staff (6 in sales), then the same company at 80 staff (12 in sales). Prices held flat at list price, billed annually. Accounting, payroll and email are the same in every path and left out.

- Path A, keep buying: monday.com Pro for every staff member ($19/seat/month), HubSpot Sales Hub Professional for sales ($90/seat/month), Zapier Team entry tier ($69/month).
  - 40 staff: $16,428 a year, $82,140 over five years. 80 staff: $32,028 a year, $160,140.
- Path B, hybrid: keep HubSpot, build a custom operations tool at the top of the cost guide's internal-tool tier ($30,000), plus 15–20% a year for five years, plus three months of monday.com and Zapier during the build.
  - 40 staff: $87,387–$94,887 (buying is $5,247–$12,747 cheaper). 80 staff: $122,067–$129,567 (hybrid saves $30,573–$38,073).
- Path C, replace all three with one custom system: the cost guide's mid-size tier ($30,000–$90,000), plus 15–20% a year for five years, plus six months of the old tools during the build.
  - 40 staff: $60,714–$188,214. 80 staff: $68,514–$196,014.
- Staff time (hypothetical hours, sourced wage): 12 hours a week of re-keying at $21.64/hour for 50 weeks = $12,984 a year. One hour a week = $1,082 a year.

Arrowbin price bands used: only the cost guide's published tiers (internal tool $5,000–$30,000, 4–10 weeks; mid-size app $30,000–$90,000, 3–6 months) and its 15–20% yearly upkeep rule. No new Arrowbin prices.

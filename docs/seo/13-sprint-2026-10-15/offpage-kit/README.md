# Off-page kit: what to do, in what order

Written 15 October 2026 for Md. Shishir Ahmed. Everything here is copy and paste: the text for each profile, the emails to clients, the LinkedIn posts and the pitches. Plan on 30 to 60 minutes a day for two weeks.

## Why this matters now

Arrowbin's site is in good shape and is about to have 15 solid guides, with an original salary data post on the way. Outside the site, there is very little:
- **2 followed referring domains**, both client footers (nbafl.net and maneelclub.com). Source: `docs/seo/12-audit-2026-10-14/backlinks.md`.
- **No claimed directory profiles.** A Clutch profile exists but is unclaimed, has 0 reviews, lists St. Petersburg as headquarters and mentions Tampa (checked 15 Oct 2026).
- **No LinkedIn company page.** The GitHub org still says "Arrowbin Digital Agency" and `info@arrowbin.com`.
- **No visible brand footprint.** On 15 October 2026 a web search for "Arrowbin" software returned nothing about Arrowbin.

Google and AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini decide what a company is, and whether to mention it, partly from what other sites say about it. Right now there is almost nothing for them to read. This kit fixes that with work only you can do: claiming profiles, asking clients and writing as the founder.

Nothing here guarantees rankings or AI mentions. These steps give Google and AI assistants the outside evidence they currently lack.

## How each kind of work helps

| Work | What it builds | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Identical profiles on Clutch, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, GoodFirms and others (`directories.md`) | **Entity consistency** | When the same name, description, founder, address and website appear on several trusted sites, and the site's `sameAs` list points to them, search engines and AI models can match them to one company with confidence. Mismatches (Tampa vs Dhaka, info@ vs hello@, "Digital Agency") make that harder. |
| Footer credits from client sites (`client-outreach.md`) | **Referring domains** | A followed link from a real business that you actually built a site for is the cleanest link you can get. Each client is one more referring domain. |
| Clutch reviews (`client-outreach.md`) | **Review signals** | Clutch category pages rank in Google for "software company in Dhaka"-type searches, and reviews decide position on them. Reviews are also third-party proof that AI answers can quote. On Clutch's Dhaka e-commerce list, some page-one firms had 1 to 14 reviews (`docs/seo/08-local/local-seo-plan.md`, 29 Sep 2026). |
| LinkedIn posts (`linkedin-posts.md`) | **Founder authority and brand mentions** | Each post puts your name, Arrowbin's name and a useful guide in front of people who may share or cite it. It also ties you, as a person, to the topics you write about. |
| Journalist requests and pitches (`pr-and-mentions.md`) | **Editorial mentions and links** | A quote in a real publication is the hardest of these signals to fake. |

## Decide these first (about 25 minutes, day 1)

Every profile asks for these, and they must be identical everywhere. Write the answers into `profile-facts.md`.

1. **Sunbiz status** (FIX-PLAN P1.2, D3): open search.sunbiz.org, look up document L24000209589, and note whether Arrowbin LLC is ACTIVE. Either way, the display name everywhere is "Arrowbin". "Arrowbin LLC" goes only in fields that ask for a legal name, and only once Sunbiz shows ACTIVE. Until then, also leave the US address off.
2. **Dhaka address** (D8): the street line as on the signage, or a decision not to publish one. And: is the office staffed and can clients visit? (That decides Google Business Profile.)
3. **Phone** (D7): confirm +880 1843-596038 is the public number, and whether it's on WhatsApp Business.
4. **Team size band, minimum project size (decision F5), hourly rate:** honest numbers. Clutch and GoodFirms ask for all three. Don't copy a minimum from the site; its published figures conflict.
5. **Discovery (decision F4):** is the discovery step free or paid? The kit only promises "a free 30-minute call" (https://cal.com/arrowbin/30min) until you decide.
6. **Client contacts:** first name and best channel for each of the 8 live clients, and which older clients you can still reach.

## What not to do

- Don't pay for links, "guest posts" or listicle placements, and don't use "submit to hundreds of directories" services.
- Don't offer anything (discounts, free work, gifts, money) for a review or a link. Google's policies ban it: https://support.google.com/business/answer/2622994 and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies.
- Don't make a footer credit part of a contract, and don't use keyword anchors like "software company Dhaka".
- Don't write reviews for clients, or ask only your happiest clients.
- Don't create a Google, Bing or Apple Maps listing at the St. Petersburg virtual office.
- Don't let profiles drift: if a fact changes (phone, address, legal status), update `profile-facts.md` first, then every profile in the tracking table.

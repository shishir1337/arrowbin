## What works on LinkedIn

- **Carousels are PDF documents.** LinkedIn shows them as swipeable slides. Ours are 1080×1350 (4:5), the size that fills the most of the feed. 8 to 12 slides works best.
- **Slide 1 does the work.** One bold claim and a reason to swipe. The last slide asks for one thing.
- **Links go in the first comment**, never the post text. Links inside a PDF can't be clicked.
- **Post on Tuesday to Thursday, 8 to 10 am US Eastern**, because US clients are the main target.
- **The Page has few followers, so its reach comes from you.** Repost every Page post from your profile with a line of your own within the first hour.

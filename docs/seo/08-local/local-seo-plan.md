## 3. Google Business Profile: eligibility and safe guidance

### What Google's rules say (quoted from [Guidelines for representing your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en))
- "If your business rents a physical mailing address but doesn't operate out of that location, also known as a virtual office, that location isn't eligible for a Business Profile."
- "Businesses can't list an office at a co-working space unless that office maintains clear signage, receives customers at the location during business hours, and is staffed during business hours by your business staff."
- Businesses must make in-person contact with customers during stated hours; online-only businesses are not eligible ([Business eligibility and ownership guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en)).
- Google increasingly asks for video verification showing signage, the workspace and staff ([BrightLocal on coworking suspensions](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/google-business-profile-for-co-working-space/), [Google community guide on coworking](https://support.google.com/business/community-guide/361385604/can-i-use-a-coworking-space-address-for-my-google-business-profile?hl=en)).

### St. Petersburg (7901 4th St N): do not create a GBP there
The address is a shared virtual office (section 0). A GBP at that address breaks the guideline above, would likely fail video verification, and risks suspension. A suspension can also cause extra scrutiny for other profiles on the same Google account. **Recommendation: no GBP for the US until there is a real, staffed space.**

Safe options for the US, in order of preference:
1. **Do nothing on Google Maps; win Tampa Bay through organic pages and directories** (Clutch, UpCity, Built In, DesignRush, Expertise.com). This is what most out-of-area competitors ranking for these terms do.
2. **If a US team member works locally**, rent a dedicated desk/office with permanent signage and posted, staffed hours that meet customers (for example at a coworking space in St. Pete), then verify there. Only do this if it is genuinely true.
3. **Service-area profile from a staffed home office**: only if a staff member actually works from a Tampa Bay home address and meets clients in person. The address is hidden; the service area is shown. Do not use the virtual office for this.

Non-Google platforms (Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp) have their own rules; Bing and Apple also verify by phone/mail/documents. Listing the virtual office as a **mailing/legal address** on B2B directories (Clutch, GoodFirms, Crunchbase, LinkedIn) is normal and fine because those are not map products.

### Review acquisition process (both markets, all platforms)
1. At project handover, send a short email asking for a review, with a direct link (GBP "Ask for reviews" link for Dhaka clients; Clutch review link for international clients).
2. Follow up once after 7 days. Never offer payment, discounts or gifts for reviews (against Google policy and FTC rules in the US).
3. Reply to every review within a week, naming the project type (not the client's private details).
4. Target: Dhaka GBP 15 reviews in 6 months, 45+ in 12 months (benchmark: TopOfStack 47). Clutch 10 reviews in 6 months (benchmark: page-one Dhaka e-commerce firms with 1 to 14 reviews).

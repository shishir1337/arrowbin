# Arrowbin brand film: "Stuck at 99%"

A 53-second brand video for winning leads. It opens on a progress bar frozen at 99%, the moment every founder burned by a developer recognises. The bar resets, then climbs through how Arrowbin works (a reply within one business day, a free 30-minute call, a fixed estimate within a week, working software every Friday), hits 100% at "launch", and ends on "Book a free call" at arrowbin.com.

# Service video 2, websites: "Three seconds"

A 53-second 16:9 film for website rebuilds, rebuilt from scratch on 26 Oct 2026 (it replaces the first "Time of death" autopsy cut). Maya, on her sofa at 2:14 pm, taps Buy now on a hand-painted vase. The next three seconds play in real time, cut on her heartbeat: 1, 2, 3, the colour drains out of her world, the line goes flat, and the tab is flicked away. "Time of death: three seconds." She is one of 53 phones in 100 that go dark (53% of mobile visits are abandoned after 3 seconds, Google). Across town the owner sees visitors but no orders. Then the film rewinds through itself, and in Arrowbin's world the page is fixed in three moves: shrink the photos, cut the plugins, rebuild every page for the phone. Same tap: the order is confirmed in a blink, well inside Google's 2.5-second target. Maya smiles, the owner's phone lights up with "New order: $64.00", and the heartbeat draws the Arrowbin mark: "Websites that don't die on arrival." Book a free call at arrowbin.com.

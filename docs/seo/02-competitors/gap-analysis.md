# Gap analysis: where Arrowbin can and cannot win

Research date 2026-09-29. It builds on `competitor-list.csv`, `competitor-profiles.md` and `winning-content-formats.md`. Statements about competitors link to sources in those files. Recommendations are judgment calls, not measured facts.

## Starting position (be honest)
- **Search and AI visibility:** Arrowbin has no first-party search data. A third-party LLM visibility test on 2026-09-29 (Babylovegrowth; 5 buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) found **0 mentions in 0 of 5 prompts**, 0 organic keywords and 0 AI Overview references for arrowbin.com. The same test showed Simform, BairesDev, Fingent and ScienceSoft as the brands AI engines recommend, with Clutch and DesignRush as the most-cited sources.
- **Content:** 3 blog posts. Competitors run far larger blogs (estimates from sitemaps): Riseup Labs about 1,066, Aalpha about 1,483, ScienceSoft about 925, Kaz about 509, Simform about 515, 2muchcoffee about 283, Sourcetoad about 158.
- **Reviews:** no Clutch profile with reviews is recorded in the brief. Competitors: Aalpha 218, Andersen 129, Saritasa 106, Simform 88, Riseup 46, SJ Innovation 39, 2muchcoffee 26, BS23 21.
- **Portfolio:** proof is mostly Bangladesh e-commerce. There are no enterprise logos, no certifications (ISO/CMMI) and no case-study metrics published yet.

## Where Arrowbin cannot realistically win (next 6 to 12 months)
1. **Head terms like "custom software development company", "software development company", "ecommerce development company" (US).** Itransition, ScienceSoft, Avenga and Luxoft hold them with decades of links, and Clutch and DesignRush own the list slots. Target these only through supporting content and directory presence.
2. **"Hire X developers" at scale.** Toptal, Lemon, Arc and BairesDev own the head terms. Riseup Labs (about 492 pages) and Space-O (62) own the long tail with volume. Arrowbin should not run a programmatic hire-page play.
3. **"Top software companies in Bangladesh".** The page-one results are self-ranking listicles from BS23, Vivasoft, Riseup, Ontik, Technext, DSi and more, plus directories. It is winnable only with a clearly better, data-backed page and links. Treat it as a medium-term bet, not a quick win.
4. **Generic "how to choose a software development company".** DesignRush, Vention and Forbes are at the top. The existing post should be kept but repositioned (see gap 7).
5. **Anything enterprise, compliance or industry-vertical at depth** (HIPAA, banking, ERP). Arrowbin has no proof in those areas, and ScienceSoft and Itransition have huge industry hubs.

## Where Arrowbin can realistically win

### Gap 1: ungated cost calculator plus transparent pricing (highest-leverage asset)
- **Competitor pattern:** every calculator we checked (ScienceSoft, Space-O, Upsilon, Vivasoft) hides the number behind an email form, and ScienceSoft promises a quote in 24 hours. No Bangladesh peer publishes prices except Aalpha's `/pricing/` pages. Locally, only Gaazzeebo publishes "from $X" prices.
- **Opportunity:** a calculator that shows a range instantly, with an optional "email me the breakdown", paired with a `/pricing` page that gives honest starting ranges per service. It matches the founder-led transparency angle and the SERP format (calculators) for "software development cost calculator", "app cost calculator" and "ecommerce website cost calculator". Numbers visible in the HTML are also more citable by AI engines.

### Gap 2: Bangladesh cost advantage with primary sources
- **Competitor pattern:** outsourcing-to-Bangladesh pages (Kaz, Vivasoft, Impala Intech, Enosis) recycle unattributed figures (salary averages, workforce sizes).
- **Opportunity:** an "Outsourcing software development to Bangladesh: rates, risks and how it works" guide.
  - Cite primary sources: BASIS, the Bangladesh ICT Division, Clutch rate filters.
  - Include an honest risks section: time zone, holidays, internet and power resilience, and the economic-instability risk that Impala Intech itself flags.
  - Explain how a US LLC contract layer (Arrowbin LLC) de-risks it.
- This framing, "US contract, Bangladesh engineering", is shared only by SJ Innovation among the peers we checked, and SJ has no llms.txt, no pricing and no hire pages.

### Gap 3: "Cost in 2026" guides with Bangladesh vs US vs Eastern Europe comparisons
- **Opportunity:** extend the existing cost post and add sibling guides:
  - MVP cost (the existing MVP timeline post can link to it)
  - ecommerce website cost
  - SaaS cost
  - mobile app cost
  - cost of maintaining software
- Each should include a regional rate table and three worked example budgets. Andersen's #1 cost page has no FAQ and no calculator, and Keyhole and Soltech are mid-authority sites, so these SERPs are contestable with a better page.

### Gap 4: e-commerce for the Bangladesh market (strongest proof area)
- **Competitor pattern:** BD e-commerce SERPs are dominated by small local shops with package pricing in taka (e.g. zaman-it.com "@20000TK", tos.com.bd, thezoomit.com) and by Brain Station 23's generic page.
- **Opportunity:** Arrowbin's live portfolio (Silent Lifestyle BD, FlexOver BD, North Bengal Agro Farms, gtegoyna.com.bd) is direct proof. Candidate pages:
  - "Ecommerce website development in Bangladesh" (English plus a Bangla version)
  - "Ecommerce website cost in Bangladesh (৳)"
  - bKash/Nagad/SSLCommerz payment integration guides
  - courier integration (Pathao, Steadfast, RedX)
  - Shopify vs WooCommerce vs custom for BD brands
- These are practical, low-competition topics. None of the international agencies covers them, and local players rarely go deep. Payment and courier integration topic volume is not measured yet and needs checking in keyword research.

### Gap 5: St. Petersburg / Tampa local pages (quick win, with a caveat)
- **Competitor pattern:** the St. Pete SERP is 7 of 9 city landing pages, many from out-of-area firms (Binary Studio, Ringo Media, Ditstek with an Indian address). Clutch's St. Pete list has only 7 firms, one of them St. Pete-based. DragonPoint ranks for Tampa from Rockledge with templated pages.
- **Opportunity:** a real St. Pete page and a Tampa page.
  - A real address and a Google Business Profile.
  - LocalBusiness + FAQPage schema.
  - Published price ranges and local proof when available.
  - Listings on Clutch, UpCity and Expertise.
- **Caveat:** 2muchcoffee uses the **same street address** (7901 4th St N, Ste 300). This is probably a shared or virtual office. Google may treat a virtual office as ineligible for a GBP or merge the listings. Check the suite number and the GBP eligibility rules before creating the profile. The founder also does not want offices emphasised, so keep these as standalone local pages that the main navigation does not push.

### Gap 6: case studies with real numbers
- **Competitor pattern:** Vivasoft, Riseup, SJ Innovation, Kaz, Aalpha and Sourcetoad publish case studies with few or no outcome metrics. Upsilon's "key numbers + named testimonial" format is the best we saw.
- **Opportunity:** when `/work/<slug>` is un-parked, use a key-numbers block, stack, timeline, team and a client quote. Only publish metrics the client confirms. E-commerce clients can supply conversion, order-volume or load-time changes.

### Gap 7: founder-led transparency and process content
- **Opportunity:** a named founder byline with a bio and LinkedIn, "how we work" pages, and a sample SOW, weekly report and handover checklist. Reposition the existing "how to choose" post as a scorecard with a downloadable RFP template.
- **Why it can win:** most competitors show a thin or anonymous byline ("Vivasoft Team", no author on the SJ Innovation and Kaz samples), while the pages that rank best show named experts (ScienceSoft, Aalpha, Space-O). E-E-A-T and AI engines both reward identifiable authors.

### Gap 8: modern-stack and "AI prototype to production" niches
- **Evidence:** Sourcetoad (Tampa) has launched `/lovable-dev-to-production-help/`, `/cursor-to-production-help/` and `/vibe-audits/`, which shows emerging demand for rescuing AI-built prototypes. No BD peer we checked targets this.
- **Opportunity:** "Next.js development company" is contested (Itransition, Halo Lab), but Next.js + e-commerce or Next.js migration pages are narrower. Arrowbin runs Next.js 16 itself.
- Search volumes are unverified and need keyword research.

### Gap 9: AI-answer visibility through the sources LLMs already cite
- AI engines cite Clutch, DesignRush, agency listicles (lowcode.agency, esparkinfo.com, gojilabs.com) and Keyhole's cost page.
- **Actions:**
  - Get a Clutch profile with reviews, even 5 to 10 would place Arrowbin in the Dhaka and St. Pete lists, where competitors have few reviews (Kaz 2, Vivasoft 9, 66Loop 6).
  - Complete DesignRush, GoodFirms and TechBehemoths profiles.
  - Pitch inclusion in third-party "top BD companies" and "affordable offshore agencies for startups" listicles.
  - Keep the llms.txt and FAQPage schema that the new build already ships. Many competitors lack one or both: ScienceSoft, Vivasoft and SJ Innovation have no llms.txt.

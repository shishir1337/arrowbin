**Gaps, confirmed today:**
- Still no editorial cost guide. Every result is a marketplace, a directory, a vendor or a generic global guide.
- Nobody shows BDT and USD side by side with a dated rate. Abbacus is USD only; bdstall and Bdtask are BDT only; Dcastalia is USD only.
- Nobody separates the build price from store fees, gateway and courier fees and upkeep.
- New angle found today: Bdtask's own Clutch profile lists $25–49 an hour, while its blog puts hourly rates at ৳1,000–৳4,000 (about $8–32). That is the two-market point in one company's own numbers. The post states both facts neutrally.
- Abbacus page "How Much Does It Cost to Hire an App Developer in Bangladesh?" gives junior $10–20, mid $20–35, senior $35–60 an hour with no source or method. Not cited.
